Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Thank you for using Selaxion. Your privacy is important to us.

What Selaxion Does

Selaxion is a Chrome extension that helps users save, organize, and manage LinkedIn candidate profiles.

Information Stored

Selaxion stores only the information that you choose to save, including:

  • Candidate names
  • LinkedIn profile URLs
  • Company names
  • Your personal notes
  • Candidate status
  • Favorite selections

Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your own device using Chrome's storage API.

Selaxion does not upload your candidate information to our servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Payment information
  • Browsing history
  • Personal analytics
  • Location data

Permissions

Selaxion requests only the permissions necessary to operate:

  • LinkedIn pages – to read profile information when you choose to save a candidate.
  • Storage – to save your candidate list locally.
  • Tabs – to identify the active LinkedIn profile.

Export Feature

When you export candidates to Excel, the file is created locally on your computer. Selaxion does not receive or store exported files.

Third-Party Services

Selaxion does not use advertising services, analytics platforms, tracking software, or third-party data collection services.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Selaxion introduces new features or changes how data is handled.
